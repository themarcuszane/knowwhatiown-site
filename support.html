Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data backed up anywhere?

No automatic backup by the app. Know What I Own stores everything on your device. (If you use iCloud Backup, iOS itself may include the app's data in your device backup; see the privacy policy.) You control the app's own backups: go to Settings → Backup, and Know What I Own will create a single backup file (in JSON format, with all photos embedded). You then decide where to save it — email it to yourself, save it to iCloud Drive, AirDrop it to another device, or keep it on a USB drive. The app never uploads or backs up your data to the cloud automatically.

How do I back up my inventory?

Open Know What I Own, open Settings, find the Backup section, and tap "Export Backup…". A backup file will be generated and presented to you via iOS's share sheet. You can then email it, save it to cloud storage, AirDrop it, or do whatever you like with the file. Keep it somewhere safe so you can restore your data if you ever need to. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who gets it can read your whole inventory, including serial numbers, values, and receipts. Avoid sending it over channels you don't trust.

How do I restore on a new phone?

Install Know What I Own on your new phone. Open the app, open Settings, find the Backup section, and tap "Import Backup…". Choose the backup file you saved earlier (from email, cloud storage, or wherever you kept it). Know What I Own will load all your properties, rooms, items, photos, and notes back onto the new device.

Why can't I add more than 25 items or a second property?

The free version of Know What I Own is limited to 1 property and up to 25 items, so you can try it out risk-free. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks unlimited properties and items. The unlock has no recurring charge. The purchase also enables CSV and PDF export for insurance reports.

How do maintenance reminders work?

You can add recurring maintenance tasks to a property. If notifications are enabled, iOS schedules a local reminder for 9 AM on each task’s due date. Manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

How do I export an insurance report?

With the one-time unlock, open a property and tap "Report." Know What I Own will generate a PDF with a cover page, a room-by-room breakdown, photos of each item, and total values. You can then share this PDF via email, Messages, or any other app on your phone. It's designed to hand directly to your insurance adjuster.
