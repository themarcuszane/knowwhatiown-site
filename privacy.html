Know What I Own

Privacy Policy

The short version: Your inventory is stored on your device. Team Drybox LLC does not operate an inventory server or collect the contents of your inventory. You choose where exported files go.

Know What I Own is an app by Team Drybox LLC. This policy is effective October 18, 2026.

Inventory Data

Properties, rooms, items, photos, receipts, notes, values, serial numbers, and maintenance tasks are stored in the app on your device. We do not operate an account or inventory-sync service, and the app does not send this content to Team Drybox LLC.

The app uses Apple's on-device storage and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell your inventory data.

Photos

You can select photos through the system photo picker. The app keeps the selected images in its local storage. It does not upload them to Team Drybox LLC.

Maintenance Reminders

If you allow notifications, iOS schedules maintenance reminders locally for 9 AM on a task's due date. You can change the app's notification permission in iOS Settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. The app checks a verified purchase entitlement through Apple. Team Drybox LLC does not receive your card number or billing details.

Exports and Backups

When you choose to export a backup, CSV file, or PDF report, the app creates the file on your device and passes it to the iOS share sheet. You decide whether to save or send it through another app or service, such as iCloud Drive or email. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. The app does not automatically sync inventory to Team Drybox LLC.

The app does not encrypt backup files, CSV files, or PDF reports. A backup contains your full inventory, including photos, receipts, serial numbers, values, and notes, and anyone who obtains the file can read it. Save or send exports only to places you trust, and protect those locations with a passcode or your own encryption.

You can import a backup using the system Files picker. If you delete the app, its local inventory is removed from that device; copies you previously saved or shared elsewhere remain wherever you put them.

Device Backups (iCloud and Computer)

If you use iCloud Backup, or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include this app's data in that backup, including your inventory, photos, and receipts. Apple's system does this, not the app, and it is governed by your Apple account settings and Apple's privacy practices. Team Drybox LLC does not receive these backups and cannot access them. You can turn iCloud Backup on or off, or choose which apps are included, in iOS Settings.

Deleting the app removes its data from your device but not from device backups you already made. Restoring a device from a backup restores the app's data along with it.

Children

Know What I Own is not designed for children under 13. Because Team Drybox LLC does not hold inventory accounts, we cannot remotely view or delete inventory stored on a device. A device owner can remove the app and manage any exported copies they saved.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date on this page will show when it was last revised.
