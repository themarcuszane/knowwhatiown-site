Why it matters

When something goes wrong, you'll be asked what you had.

After a fire, a theft, or a flood, the hardest part is often the list. Which TV, which model, what did it cost, do you still have the receipt? A record made while everything is still in place turns a difficult conversation into an organized one.

  • What

    What it was

    Name, brand, model, and serial number for each item, so there's no guessing later.

  • Worth

    What it was worth

    Purchase price and your own estimate of current value, added up for each room and for the whole property.

  • Proof

    How you can show it

    A photo of the item, a photo of the receipt, and the warranty date, kept together with the item.

Know What I Own doesn't file claims or promise any outcome. It helps you keep a clear record you can hand to your insurer or agent.

How it works

Three steps, at your own pace.

  1. 01

    Add a property and its rooms

    Start with a home, a storage unit, or a vacation property. Then add rooms the way the place is actually laid out. The app suggests common ones like Living Room, Kitchen, and Garage.

  2. 02

    Add what's in them

    Pick a photo, note the brand, model, and serial number, and add the purchase date, price, and your estimate of its current value. Attach a photo of the receipt and a warranty date if you have them.

  3. 03

    Share or back up when you choose

    Create a PDF insurance report or a CSV file, or export a single backup file. Nothing is sent anywhere until you send it. (PDF and CSV come with the one-time unlock.)

Private by design

No account. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.

You're documenting your most personal belongings, so the app asks for nothing it doesn't need.

Maintenance

Upkeep, logged as you go.

Homes need regular attention: filters, smoke detectors, gutters, the water heater. Add recurring tasks, mark them done, and the app keeps the history and reminds you when the next one is due.

  • Reminders arrive at 9 AM on the day a task is due. They're scheduled on your device by iOS, if you allow notifications.
  • Mark a task done to log today's date.
  • Your upkeep history appears in the PDF report as a maintenance-log page.

Questions

Straight answers.

Where is my data stored?

In the app, on your device. There is no account and no sign-up, and Team Drybox LLC does not run an inventory server, so we never receive your list. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in those device backups; the privacy policy explains this.

How do I back it up?

Open Settings, then Backup, and tap “Export Backup…”. The app creates a single file with your items, photos, receipts, and maintenance tasks, and hands it to the iOS share sheet so you can save it wherever you trust, such as iCloud Drive. “Import Backup…” reads it back. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who gets it can read your inventory.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Your inventory lives on the phone, and we don't have a copy, so a saved backup file is your safety net: install the app on a new device and import it. If you also use iOS device backups, restoring a new device from one may bring the app's data back too. Without either, the data on a lost phone can't be recovered by us.

What does the one-time unlock include?

Unlimited properties and items, the PDF insurance report, and CSV export. Without it you can use the app with 1 property and up to 25 items. It's a single purchase with no subscription, handled by Apple, and “Restore Purchase” in Settings brings it back on a reinstall or a new device.

Does it need an internet connection?

No, not for your inventory. Items, photos, search, maintenance tasks, reports, exports, and backups all work offline. A connection is only needed to download the app and to buy or restore the unlock through the App Store.

How do I add photos? Is there a camera in the app?

You choose photos from your photo library with the standard iOS photo picker; the app has no built-in camera, so take pictures with the Camera app first. The app stores reduced-size copies and leaves your originals untouched.

Will the report get my claim approved?

No app can promise that. The PDF report is a clear, organized record of what you own, what it's worth to you, and what you can show for it. What an insurer accepts depends on your policy and your insurer, so ask your agent what they want to see.

Is there an Android version?

No. Know What I Own is built for iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and also runs on iPad. There is no Android or web version.
