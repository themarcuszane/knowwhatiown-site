How can we help?

Know What I Own is designed to be straightforward to use. Below are answers to common questions. For anything else, please reach out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data backed up anywhere?

No automatic backup by the app. Know What I Own stores everything on your device. (If you use iCloud Backup, iOS itself may include the app's data in your device backup; see the privacy policy.) You control the app's own backups: go to Settings → Backup, and Know What I Own will create a single backup file (in JSON format, with all photos embedded). You then decide where to save it — email it to yourself, save it to iCloud Drive, AirDrop it to another device, or keep it on a USB drive. The app never uploads or backs up your data to the cloud automatically.

How do I back up my inventory?

Open Know What I Own, open Settings (the gear icon), find the Backup section, and tap “Export Backup…”. A backup file will be generated and presented to you via iOS's share sheet. The file includes your properties, rooms, items, photos, receipts, and maintenance tasks. You can then email it, save it to cloud storage, AirDrop it, or do whatever you like with the file. Keep it somewhere safe so you can restore your data if you ever need to. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who gets it can read your whole inventory, including serial numbers, values, and receipts. Avoid sending it over channels you don't trust.

How do I restore on a new phone?

Install Know What I Own on your new phone. Open the app, open Settings, find the Backup section, and tap “Import Backup…”. Choose the backup file you saved earlier (from email, cloud storage, or wherever you kept it). Know What I Own will load your properties, rooms, items, photos, receipts, and maintenance tasks back onto the new device. A property that is already on the device is skipped rather than duplicated. If you bought the one-time unlock, tap “Restore Purchase” in Settings to bring it back; the purchase is tracked by Apple, not in the backup file.

Why can't I add more than 25 items or a second property?

The free version of Know What I Own is limited to 1 property and up to 25 items, so you can try it out before you buy. A single one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks unlimited properties and items. The unlock has no recurring charge. The purchase also enables CSV and PDF export for insurance reports.

How do I add photos?

When you add or edit an item, tap “Add Photo” or “Add Receipt” and choose an image from your photo library with the standard iOS photo picker. The app has no built-in camera, so take the picture with the Camera app first. Know What I Own keeps a reduced-size copy of each photo in the app and does not change the original in your Photos library.

How do maintenance reminders work?

You can add recurring maintenance tasks to a property. If notifications are enabled, iOS schedules a local reminder for 9 AM on each task’s due date, showing the task and property name. Manage notification permission in iOS Settings.

How do I export an insurance report?

With the one-time unlock, open the Inventory tab and tap “Report.” Know What I Own will generate a PDF with a cover page showing the total estimated value, a room-by-room listing (with a small photo of each item that has one, plus brand, model, serial number, warranty date, and value), and a maintenance-log page if you have maintenance tasks. Receipt photos are not placed in the PDF; the report notes which items have a receipt on file. You can then share the PDF via email, Messages, or any other app on your phone.

How do I export a CSV?

With the one-time unlock, open the Inventory tab and tap “CSV.” You get a spreadsheet file of the property’s inventory (room, item, category, brand and model, serial number, purchase date and price, estimated value, warranty date, and notes) to share or save.

Does the app need an internet connection?

Not for your inventory. Everything except the purchase works offline. You need a connection to download the app and to buy or restore the unlock through the App Store.
